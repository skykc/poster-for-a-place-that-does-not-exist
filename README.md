A travel poster for **VELMARA** — a place that does not exist. A chain of
floating islands drifts above a sea of clouds at golden hour, and the
Sky-Lighthouse sweeps its beam across the clouds as the sun sets.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) draws the poster procedurally,
pixel by pixel, and writes it deterministically to `/output/entry.png`
(1024×1024, under 1 MiB).

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

The program writes `entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set,
otherwise into `/output`.

## The place

VELMARA is an archipelago that never touches the ground: green-topped
islands float over a warm sea of clouds, and the only way to find your way
home after sunset is the beam of the Sky-Lighthouse. Come for the light,
stay for the quiet above the clouds.
